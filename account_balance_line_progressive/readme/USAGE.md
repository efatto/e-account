1.  If you're an accounting adviser, enable developer mode and go to
    Accounting \> Adviser \> Journal items.
2.  Or open an account or a partner, and click on Action \> Journal
    items.
3.  You will see a column named "Progressive Balance" with the balance
    of the line.
4.  If multi-currency is enabled, you will see a column named
    "Progressive Balance Currency" with the currency balance of the
    line.

If the account selected is not of type payable or receivable, the amount
is shown without regarding the partner.

Viceversa, every account of type payable or receivable show on the rows
the progressive balance for the partner of the row, not the total
progress balance of the account.
